How to find them

Move
WASD or arrows. On a phone, drag the left half of the screen.
Tag
Click straight on a hider while you're close to it. Miss, and the sweep clock loses 4 seconds.
Ping
Space fires a sonar ring. Anything concealed inside it flashes red for a moment. Costs charge.
Focus
Hold Shift to slow down and sharpen your optics. Camouflage seams get much easier to read up close.
As hider
F paint · R pose · Space eyedropper · C crouch · E wall-stick · T taunt · Enter lock in.
Playing together
Pass & Play works offline on one device: each player hides in turn, the last one seeks. Online uses a room code between browsers.
Tells
Watch for a flat patch of floor, a slow shimmer crossing a surface, or a single eye-blink. Listen for the ticking too.
Movers
Scout-class hiders relocate mid-sweep if you never come near them. A patch of floor that shifted is a patch worth clicking.
